🏀 3D Hoops

Third-person street ball. Real arcs, real rims, real dunks.

0
Mode
Teams

Full court runs both ways with two hoops, first to 21. Pick a team size (your side first, so 2v1 is you + a teammate against one defender). You stay on one player the whole game; your teammates are CPU bots that cut, pass and defend, and each one has their own look. G passes to the teammate you are aiming at. 2P versus: Player 1 uses WASD / Space / E / G, Player 2 uses the arrow keys / Enter / slash / comma, on one shared screen with the camera framing both of you (drag to rotate it, wheel to zoom). 2P co-op: both of you are on the same team against the CPU.

CPU level

WASD / arrows move (the camera follows you around the hoop). Shift sprint. Hold Space to charge a shot and let go with the bar in the green. F crossover. R pump fake (a defender who jumps at it is out of the play). E steal, block or contest. Drag to look around, wheel to zoom, C for a locked free camera, double-click to reset. Sprint at the rim and tap Space to dunk. Outside the arc is worth 3, and the farther out you shoot, the narrower the green zone. In a Full game there are four quarters, a 24-second shot clock, a halftime break, overtime if it is tied, and two timeouts each (T).
